# Election Maps

A collection of precinct-level election maps created using ArcGIS Pro.

Maps are created using publicly available election returns and geographic boundary datasets.

## Maps

### 2026 Harris County Judge Election

Precinct-level visualization of the 2026 Harris County Judge election between Letitia Plummer (D) and Orlando Sanchez (R).

Geographic coverage: Harris County, Texas

Election data: Harris County Clerk's Office, Elections Department

Precinct boundaries: Harris County VPCTs_2026

Software: ArcGIS Pro

Visualization: Winning candidate's two-party vote share by precinct

### 2026 Bexar County Judge Election

Precinct-level visualization of the 2026 Bexar County Judge election between Ron Nirenberg (D) and Patrick Von Dohlen (R).

Geographic coverage: Bexar County, Texas

Election data: Bexar County Elections Department (2026)

Precinct boundaries: Bexar County GIS, Voting Precincts (2026)

Software: ArcGIS Pro

Visualization: Winning candidate's two-party vote share by precinct

### 2026 Michigan's 10th Congressional District Election

Precinct-level visualization of the 2026 U.S. House election in Michigan's 10th Congressional District between Christina Hines (D) and Michael Bouchard (R).

Geographic coverage: Michigan's 10th Congressional District (Macomb and Oakland Counties)

Election data: Macomb County Clerk; Oakland County Clerk

Precinct boundaries: Michigan Department of State, 2026 Voting Precincts

Software: ArcGIS Pro

Visualization: Winning candidate's share of total votes cast by precinct
