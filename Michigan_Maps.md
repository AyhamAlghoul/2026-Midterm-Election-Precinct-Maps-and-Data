# Michigan Maps

## 2026 Election in Michigan's 10th Congressional Election
![2026 Michigan 10th Congressional District Map](Election-Maps/Michigans%27s%2010th%20District.png)
Former Wayne and Washtenaw County prosecutor Christina Hines (D) defeated former U.S. Army Captain Michael Bouchard (R) in Michigan's 10th Congressional District in the 2026 U.S. House election by 7.17 percentage points, receiving 52.44% of the districtwide vote.

The result represented a 13.81-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2024 presidential election, when Donald Trump carried the district by 6.64 percentage points, and a 13.29-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2024 U.S. House election, when Republican incumbent John James carried the district by 6.12 percentage points.
