# 2026 Texas Maps

## 2026 Harris County Judge Elections
![2026 Harris County Judge Election Map](Election-Maps/HCJ_2026_Map.png)

Former Houston City Councilmember Letitia Plummer (D) defeated former Harris County Treasurer Orlando Sanchez (R) in the 2026 Harris County Judge election by 21.90 percentage points, receiving 60.94% of the countywide vote.

The result represented a 16.37-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2024 presidential election and a 20.3-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2022 Harris County Judge election.
## 2026 Bexar County Judge Elections
![2026 Harris County Judge Election Map](Election-Maps/Bexar_County_Judge.png)

Former San Antonio Mayor Ron Nirenberg (D) defeated Patrick Von Dohlen (R) in the 2026 Bexar County Judge election by 33.10 percentage points, receiving 66.55% of the vote.

The result represented a 23.48-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2024 presidential election and a 15.10-percentage-point Democratic shift relative to the 2022 Bexar County Judge election.
